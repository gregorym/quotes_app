# No Excuses Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

No Excuses is designed to work without an account. Your name, goals, selected focus areas, friction and pressure preferences, reminder schedule, onboarding status, and saved favorites are stored locally on your device. No Excuses does not sell this information.

The app requests notification permission only when you choose to enable reminders. Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your operating system. The Home Screen widget reads only the quote database bundled with the app.

Subscription purchases and entitlement status are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles the associated payment and account information under its own privacy policy. No Excuses does not receive your complete payment-card details.

The app may request its remote quote database over HTTPS. That server may receive routine network information such as an IP address and user agent. If the request fails, the app uses its bundled offline quote database. Choosing Share opens the operating system share sheet; information is sent only to the destination you select.

Local information remains until you remove it or uninstall the app. No Excuses is not directed to children under 13.

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project’s [public support tracker](https://github.com/gregorym/quotes_app/issues).
